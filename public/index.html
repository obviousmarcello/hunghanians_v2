    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I register?

    Click "Register now", fill in the short form and you'll get a confirmation email with your reference number and payment details.

    How and when do I pay?

    By bank transfer within 5 days of registering. Please put your reference number in the transfer comment so we can match your payment.

    How much does it cost for children?

    Children aged 6–14 pay the reduced fee. Let us know about younger children in the form so we can plan for them too.

    Can I change or cancel my registration?

    Yes — just reply to your confirmation email or use the contact form below, and mention your reference number.
